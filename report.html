Report a bug

Found something that doesn't work as expected? Send the details to the jeSDR development team. Clear steps and a screenshot make issues much easier to reproduce and fix.

Before submitting: Please check that you're running the latest jeSDR release. Include the exact version, what you were doing, what you expected to happen, and what actually happened. Please do not include passwords, private keys, or other sensitive information.
