Some values are kept by the radio but not shown in the menu: the band stacks (BS80M…BS10M), each mode group's filter (FLT_SSB, FLT_CW, …), the equalizer band frequencies and Q (RXEQ1F…TXEQ5Q), RIT and XIT (RIT, RIT_OFS, XIT, XIT_OFS, set from the Quick page), and the Quick page's list (QUICK).

Display settings (theme, waterfall contrast, scope colors, waterfall speed, ribbons, spots, on-screen keyboard) and the browser's microphone and listening choices are kept in each browser, not on the radio.
